Released versions of `lennix-frontend`. What each surface does is in

## 1.0.0

First release of the Lennix web app: the AEC experience on top of
`lennix-ms-bk` — schedule import, the dependency graph, the critical path and
propagated delay, budget and progress, and the role-scoped surfaces around
them. The full surface inventory is in [`docs/PRODUCT.md`](docs/PRODUCT.md).

The repository leaves its open-source scaffolding behind with this release:
